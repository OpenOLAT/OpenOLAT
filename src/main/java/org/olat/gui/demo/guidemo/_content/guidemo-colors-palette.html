Greys

The neutral scale: 18 greys from 0 (white) to 1000 (black), var(--o-neutral-{step}) of the current theme. Steps 50 to 900 have the luminance of the same step of the hues below. Each cell shows the hex value and the contrast on white. A theme tints the scale with $o-neutral-hue (OKLCH hue, default 260, slate) and $o-neutral-chroma (default 0.038, 0 gives pure greys); the lightness of each step stays. The tab "Tokens" shows which token reads which step.

Colors

14 hues with 10 steps each. Every step of a hue has the same relative luminance as the same step of the other hues, so a step number stands for the same lightness in every hue. Each cell shows the hex value and the contrast of its text color. The cell color is var(--o-{hue}-{step}) of the current theme. The tab "Swatches" shows which step a swatch role uses. Below the 14 hues, separated, are the theme colors: the brand colors of the current theme, generated from $o-theme-colors (default $brand-primary) with the same rule. They change with the theme; the 14 hues do not.

Rules

  • Steps 50 to 400 are tints. Their text is step 900 of the same hue (8:1 and more).
  • Steps 500 to 900 are shades. Their text is white (4.85:1 and more). Step 500 is also the first step that reaches 4.5:1 as text on white.
  • Dead zone (dashed line): between 400 and 500 the scale skips the lightness where neither white nor dark text reaches 4.5:1.
  • Bright hues (yellow, orange, lime, green, cyan) use step 400 as solid fill with dark text. A yellow with white text reads as brown.
  • Deep hues (all others) use step 500 as solid fill with white text.
  • Theme colors use the brand color itself as solid fill ("solid brand"): it replaces the step with the same text polarity and the nearest luminance. A brand color where neither white nor dark text reaches 4.5:1 uses the generated step 500. Tint is step 300 and light is step 200 (200 and 100 for a solid fill of 300), dark is two steps darker than the solid fill.
  • Contrast rule: 4.5:1 for every text on every fill, also for large and bold text. The theme compile fails when a pair is below.
