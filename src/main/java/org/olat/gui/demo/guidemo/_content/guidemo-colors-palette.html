Greys

The neutral scale: 18 greys from 0 (white) to 1000 (black), var(--o-neutral-{step}) of the current theme. Steps 50 to 900 have the luminance of the same step of the hues below. Each cell shows the hex value and the contrast on white. A theme tints the scale with $o-neutral-hue (OKLCH hue, default 260, slate) and $o-neutral-chroma (default 0.038, 0 gives pure greys); the lightness of each step stays. The tab "Tokens" shows which token reads which step.

Colors

14 hues with 10 text steps and the fill step 450 each. Every text step of a hue has the same relative luminance as the same step of the other hues, so a step number stands for the same lightness in every hue. Each cell shows the hex value and the contrast of its text color. Step 450 (dashed outline) is the midpoint of step 400 and step 500 in OKLab: a fill or shape color only, no text color, no swatch role, no chooser color. The cell color is var(--o-{hue}-{step}) of the current theme. The tab "Swatches" shows which step a swatch role uses. Below the 14 hues, separated, are the theme colors: the brand colors of the current theme, generated from $o-theme-colors (default $brand-primary) with the same rule. They change with the theme; the 14 hues do not.

Rules

  • Steps 50 to 400 are tints. Their text is step 900 of the same hue (8:1 and more).
  • Steps 500 to 900 are shades. Their text is white (4.85:1 and more). Step 500 is also the first step that reaches 4.5:1 as text on white.
  • Step 450 (dashed outline): the fill step between 400 and 500, the midpoint of the two steps in OKLab. White text on it has 3.02:1 to 3.06:1, the text of step 900 5.18:1 to 5.27:1, as a mark on white 3.02:1 to 3.06:1. A fill or shape color only (charts, markers, borders); no swatch role, no chooser color, no automatic step choice reads it.
  • All hues use step 500 as solid fill with white text and step 700 as dark fill. Tint is step 300, light step 200, soft step 50.
  • Yellow is the exception: step 500 of yellow with white text reads as ochre. Its solid fill is step 300 with dark text, dark 500 (white text), tint 200, light 100, soft 50.
  • Borders are near their fill: one step darker than the fill (soft 100, light 300, tint 400, solid 600, dark 800; yellow soft 100, light 200, tint 300, solid 400, dark 600). They have no contrast rule on white, the text on the fill carries the contrast.
  • Theme colors use the brand color itself as solid fill ("solid brand"): it replaces the step with the same text polarity and the nearest luminance. A brand color where neither white nor dark text reaches 4.5:1 uses the generated step 500. Tint is step 300 and light is step 200 (200 and 100 for a solid fill of 300), dark is two steps darker than the solid fill.
  • Contrast rule: 4.5:1 for every text on every fill, also for large and bold text. The theme compile fails when a pair is below.
